Supply Capabilities

Comprehensive Material Logistics

From single foundation pours to multi-month mega highway projects, our dedicated fleet and certified supply yards keep your job site moving.

Bulk Dumper Fleet Delivery

Dedicated fleet of 10-wheeler and 12-wheeler dumpers operating 24 hours a day with scheduled site drop-offs.

  • On-demand day and night transit
  • Computerized load slips provided
  • GPS fleet monitoring

Certified Material Grading

Every consignment is batch-sampled and laboratory tested for silt content, organic impurities, and flakiness index.

  • IS 383 & IS 2386 compliance
  • Silt percentage < 3.0% control
  • Test certificates on request

RMC Batching Plant Supply

High-volume continuous aggregate and manufactured sand contracts for Ready-Mix Concrete batching facilities.

  • Continuous stockpile replenishment
  • Priority peak-hour dispatch
  • Competitive wholesale tonnage

Highway & Infrastructure Sub-Base

MoRTH compliant Granular Sub Base (GSB) and Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) for highways, expressways, and logistics parks.

  • MoRTH Table 400-1 approved
  • High CBR compaction strength
  • Pre-mixed quarry supply

Precast & RCC Aggregates

Clean 10mm and 20mm blue granite stone aggregates specifically crushed for precast concrete components and dense reinforcement.

  • Cubic shape with low flakiness
  • High compressive bonding strength
  • Zero deleterious foreign material

Weighbridge Integrity

100% transparent computerized weighing at verified weighbridges ensuring exact tonnage delivered to your construction site.

  • Zero weight discrepancies
  • Tare and Gross printed slips
  • Fair commercial pricing
